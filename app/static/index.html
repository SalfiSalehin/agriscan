AgriScan

Check a leaf for disease

Upload one clear photo of a leaf. AgriScan names the most likely disease and explains why it happens and what you can do.

1. Which crop? (picking it gives better answers)
2. Add a photo

Best: one leaf, close up, in daylight and in focus.

AgriScan is a student portfolio project. The model learned mostly from studio-style photos, so it is less reliable on photos taken in the field, and it knows only 19 conditions of four crops. Use it as a first hint, not as a final answer.

Photos: PlantVillage and PlantDoc (CC BY 4.0). Advice is summarised from university extension services; see the sources under each result.
